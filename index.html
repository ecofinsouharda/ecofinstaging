About Ecofin

Rooted in Hubli.
Built on trust.

Ecofin Credit Souharda Sahakari Niyamit is a member-owned cooperative credit society based in Hubli, dedicated to bringing honest, accessible financial services to individuals, families, and small businesses.

We believe banking should feel personal. That's why our members are not just customers — they are partners in a shared financial future, guided by cooperative principles of transparency and mutual growth.

Chairman's Message

Welcome to Ecofin Credit Souharda

At Ecofin, our commitment is to provide trustworthy, transparent, and member-focused financial services that empower individuals, families, and businesses. Guided by cooperative principles of integrity, accountability, and service, we strive to build lasting relationships based on trust and mutual growth.

Together, we will continue building a stronger and more prosperous future for every family in Hubli.

We remain dedicated to innovation, financial inclusion, and delivering value to every member. I sincerely thank our members, customers, employees, and well-wishers for their continued confidence and support.
